# Privacy Notice

**Version 13** · Effective November 2, 2026

This Privacy Notice explains how Drifted (the "**Operator**," "**we**," "**us**") collects, uses, stores and shares information in connection with the Branch application and its related services (the "**Service**"). Contact: **drifted1000@outlook.com**.

The Service does not require an account (creator accounts are optional), does not display advertising, and does not use analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies.

## 1. Information Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored locally within the application on your device and is not transmitted to the Operator: your favorites, recently viewed Mods, display preferences, a record that you accepted the Terms of Use, the secret Community Forum key described in Section 5, your profile picture (unless you have a creator account), the Mods on your Watchlist, the location of your Gorilla Tag folder if you link it in the Mod manager, which Mods you downloaded and when (used only to ask you to rate them later), and which announcements and polls you have already seen. You may clear favorites and history in Settings, and all locally stored information is removed when you uninstall the application. Files you download are saved only to the location you choose.

## 2. Information Processed by the Service

**2.1 Technical request data.** When the application communicates with the Service, our server necessarily receives your Internet Protocol (IP) address and basic request information.

**2.2 Hashed identifiers.** To prevent abuse and enforce usage limits, the Service converts your IP address into a one-way, keyed cryptographic hash (a "**Hashed Identifier**"). The IP address itself is not stored in our database.

**2.3 Rate limiting.** Short-term counters associated with Hashed Identifiers are held in server memory and are discarded when the server restarts.

**2.3A Automatic blocking.** If a connection sends a flood of requests, for example hundreds a minute, or keeps sending uploads or mod checks after being told to slow down, the Service blocks its Hashed Identifier for twenty-four (24) hours. We store the Hashed Identifier, the reason and the time, never the IP address itself. You can ask us to review a block at drifted1000@outlook.com.

**2.4 Download statistics.** When you download a Mod or open its download page, we record the Hashed Identifier, the release and the date so that each person is counted once per day. These records are deleted after thirty (30) days; only aggregate totals are retained.

**2.5 Server logs.** Our server logs request paths, response status and timing for security and troubleshooting. These logs do not include passwords, the content of reports or uploads, or contact information.

## 3. User Submissions (Mod Uploads)

When you upload a Mod, we store and publish the Mod file, its name, description and any optional details you provide (such as an author name, version, category or source link). If you choose to remain anonymous, the author is displayed as "Anonymous." We also store the Hashed Identifier associated with the upload and the results of the automated safety check, which are visible only to the Operator and are used to enforce upload limits and to address abuse. Published information is publicly available to all users of the Service. This information is retained for as long as the Mod remains on the Service and is deleted when the Mod is removed, subject to routine backups.

## 4. The Mod Checker

When you submit a file to the Mod Checker or upload a Mod:

(a) the file is processed in memory on our server. Files submitted only to the Mod Checker are not stored;

(b) our server extracts technical indicators from the file, such as function names, embedded links and archive file names. These indicators, and not the file itself, may be transmitted to our artificial-intelligence provider, SiliconFlow, for analysis. SiliconFlow processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not submit files that contain personal information;

(c) the analysis result (score, summary and reasons) is cached together with the file's SHA-256 fingerprint for up to thirty (30) days so that identical files need not be analyzed again. Cached results are not linked to the person who submitted the file; and

(d) to enforce daily limits, we keep a usage count per Hashed Identifier, which is deleted after seven (7) days.

**Ask Branch.** When you send a message to Ask Branch, your message and the earlier messages of the same conversation are transmitted, together with information about the app, the listed Mods and the most recent Player Tracker data, to SiliconFlow to generate a reply. Branch does not store the content of your conversations; it is kept only in the app until you close it or start a new chat. To enforce the free-use limit, we record the Hashed Identifier and the time of each message, and delete these records after one (1) day. Do not include personal information in your messages.

## 5. Community Forum and Chat

The Community Forum does not use accounts. When you first open it, the application generates a random secret key, which is stored only on your device. Our server stores a one-way hash of that key (never the key itself), a randomly generated display name that you may change, the Hashed Identifier of the connection that created it, and whether posting has been restricted. Your posts, replies, chat messages, display name and a short identifier derived from the hashed key are public. Chat messages are deleted automatically after thirty (30) days. Other Forum Content is retained until it is removed by the Operator; removed posts are replaced with a placeholder. Reports about Forum Content are visible only to the Operator and are deleted one hundred eighty (180) days after they are closed. Deleting the key from your device (for example, by uninstalling the application) does not delete posts you have already made; contact us to request removal.
## 6. Rewards Program

If you apply for the Rewards Program, we store your Roblox username, the link to your Payout Item, the application date, the result of the automated review of your uploads, the Operator's decision, and a record of each Reward credited, paid or voided, linked to your Community Forum identity. To check that uploads are unique, we store a fingerprint of each uploaded Mod's code (the names of its classes and methods) and compare new uploads against it; information about your uploads may be sent to SiliconFlow for this review. Rewards information is visible only to the Operator and is used to run the program and deliver Rewards. It is retained while the program is running and for up to one (1) year afterwards for record keeping, after which it is deleted. You may ask us to delete it sooner, which ends your participation.
## 7. Reports and Mod Requests

If you submit a report or a mod request, we store the related Mod, the reason or details you provide, your contact information if you choose to provide it, the Hashed Identifier and the time of submission. This information is visible only to the Operator and is deleted one hundred eighty (180) days after the report or request is closed.

**Turning off AI.** You can turn off Branch AI at any time in Settings → Privacy. While it is off, the app tells our server not to use artificial intelligence for anything you do: your files are checked with the basic scan only, your uploads, rewards applications and mod suggestions are not sent to SiliconFlow, Ask Branch and easy upload are unavailable, and previously cached AI results are not shown to you.

**Setup questions.** The optional setup shown when you first open the app may ask where you heard about Branch and what you want to use it for. If you answer, we only add one to an anonymous total for each answer; your answers are not linked to you, your device or your Hashed Identifier. If you pick mod interests, they are sent together with the list of Mods to our artificial-intelligence provider, SiliconFlow, to suggest a few Mods, and are not stored by Branch. Your choices are also saved on your computer so the app remembers that setup is finished.

**Inbox.** Reports sent from the app are linked to your Community Forum identity (the random key stored on your computer, as described in Section 5) so that you can follow the report and read the Operator's reply in the Inbox. Only you can see your reports and their replies; the Operator's private notes are never shown. The Inbox also shows announcements and update notes, which are the same for everyone. Which items you have already seen is stored only on your computer.

**Watchlist.** When you look up a GitHub link on the Watchlist page, or the app checks the Mods you watch for new versions, the GitHub links are sent to our server, which asks GitHub for the newest release. We do not store which links you look up or watch, and they are not linked to you; GitHub sees only our server. The list of Mods you watch, and which versions you have seen, is stored only on your computer.

**Creator hub and GitHub updates.** The Creator hub shows statistics about Mods you uploaded, worked out from the download and rating records described above. If a Mod you uploaded has a GitHub link, our server checks that GitHub repository for new releases about every thirty (30) minutes and, when one appears, adds it to your Mod after the automatic safety check described in Section 4. You can turn this off for each Mod in the Creator hub. Messages about these updates are linked to your Community Forum identity, shown in your Inbox, and deleted after ninety (90) days.

**Profile picture.** The first time you open Branch, you get a profile picture made of the Branch logo on a random colour. You can pick another colour or upload your own picture, which the app crops and shrinks to a small image on your computer before using it. Without a creator account your profile picture is saved only on your computer. With a creator account it is saved to your account (see below). Only upload pictures you have the right to use, and don't upload pictures of other people without their permission.

**Creator accounts.** Creator accounts are optional. If you make one, we store the username you choose, your profile picture (a colour, or the small picture you uploaded), a salted Argon2 hash of your password (never the password itself), when the account was made and when it last logged in, and a SHA-256 hash of the community key of each computer that is logged in to it. We do not ask for an email address or any other personal information. Mods you upload while logged in are linked to your account so you can edit them. Logging out removes that computer's login. To delete your account, email us at drifted1000@outlook.com.

**Rating reminders.** When you download or install a Mod, the app remembers the Mod and the date on your computer so it can ask you to rate it about two days later. This record is not sent to us. If you rate the Mod, the rating is handled as described under Ratings.

**Pop-ups.** The Operator can show an announcement or poll in a pop-up when you open the app. Which pop-ups you have already seen is stored only on your computer. Votes cast in a pop-up poll are handled as described under Polls.

**Mod manager.** If you link your Gorilla Tag folder, the app reads the mod files in your BepInEx plugins folder on your computer. The folder location is saved only on your computer. To show which of your mods are on Branch and which have updates, the app sends the SHA-256 checksums (fingerprints) of those files to our server; the files themselves and their names are not sent, and the checksums are not stored. A file is only uploaded if you press Scan, in which case it is handled like any other Mod Checker upload (Section 4). Turning a mod off renames it on your computer, and removing it moves it to a backup folder; nothing is deleted.

**Videos and screenshots.** Uploaders can add screenshots and a YouTube video to a Mod. The video's preview picture is loaded through our server, so YouTube does not see you just for opening a Mod page. Only when you press play is the video loaded from YouTube (using its privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com player), and from then on YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that video.

**Polls.** When you vote in a poll in the Community tab, we store your vote together with the Hashed Identifier so that each person can vote once and change their vote. Polls are only shown as totals, and votes are deleted when the poll is deleted.

**Ratings.** When you rate a Mod, we store your star rating together with the Hashed Identifier so that each person can rate a Mod only once and can change their rating later. Ratings are shown only as averages and counts, and are deleted if the Mod is removed.

**Uploads through Discord.** When you post a mod file in the upload channel of the Branch Discord server, our bot downloads the file, processes it as described in Section 4, and publishes it. We do not store your Discord username or ID; we store only a scrambled (hashed) version of your Discord user ID, linked to your uploads, to enforce the daily upload limit. Any name you write in the message is used as the author name; otherwise the upload is shown as Anonymous.

**Player Tracker (Beta).** The Player Tracker displays information about public Gorilla Tag rooms received from a third-party Discord bot: room codes, regions, and in-game player names, IDs and colors. Branch does not collect this information from you or your device. We keep only the fifty (50) most recent room reports and discard anything older. If you would like information about your in-game account removed from the Player Tracker, contact us at the address below.

## 8. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on the following providers to operate the Service: **Railway** (server hosting and database storage), **Roblox** (where Rewards are delivered by purchasing your Payout Item), **Discord** (the Player Tracker reads room reports from a Discord channel), **SiliconFlow** (artificial-intelligence analysis for the Mod Checker, upload safety checks and Ask Branch; information extracted from submitted files, and for uploads the Mod name and description, may be sent to SiliconFlow) and **GitHub** (app updates are downloaded from GitHub; when you choose to view a Mod's source code, the application retrieves it directly from GitHub; Watchlist lookups and the automatic GitHub updates are made by our server, so GitHub sees our server rather than you). When you press play on a Mod's video, **YouTube** loads it under its own privacy policy. Each provider processes information under its own terms and policies and may keep its own logs. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising purposes.

## 10. Children

The Service is intended for users aged thirteen (13) and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users under 13 must not provide contact information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.
