# Copyright and Takedown Policy

**Effective October 29, 2026**

Drifted (the "**Operator**") respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users of Branch to do the same. Branch hosts Mods uploaded by users. This policy explains how rights holders may request removal of material and how uploaders may respond.

## 1. Submitting a Takedown Notice

If you believe that material available on Branch infringes your copyright or other rights, send a written notice to **drifted1000@outlook.com** (or use the "Report" button in the application and select "Copyright or permission issue") that includes:

1. your full name and contact information, including an email address;
2. identification of the copyrighted work or other right you claim has been infringed;
3. identification of the material on Branch that you claim is infringing, including the Mod's name and, if possible, its link;
4. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent or the law;
5. a statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the rights holder or are authorized to act on the rights holder's behalf; and
6. your physical or electronic signature.

Incomplete notices may not be actionable. Submitting a notice that you know to be false may expose you to liability.

## 2. Our Response

Upon receiving a complete notice, the Operator will review it and, where appropriate, promptly remove or disable access to the identified material. The Operator may disable a download or unpublish a listing while a notice is under review. Where possible, the Operator will make reasonable efforts to inform the uploader.

## 3. Counter-Notice

If you uploaded material that was removed and you believe in good faith that it was removed by mistake or misidentification, or that you have the right to distribute it, you may send a counter-notice to **drifted1000@outlook.com** that includes:

1. your name and contact information;
2. identification of the material that was removed and where it appeared before removal;
3. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief that the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification; and
4. your physical or electronic signature.

The Operator may restore the material at its discretion, and may share the counter-notice with the person who submitted the original notice.

## 4. Repeat Infringers

The Operator may, in appropriate circumstances and at its sole discretion, restrict or block users who repeatedly upload infringing material.

## 5. Distribution Permission

Crediting an author does not by itself grant permission to redistribute their work. Users who upload a Mod must confirm that they created it or have the right to distribute it. Where permission to host a copy is unclear, the Operator links to the author's original release instead of hosting a copy.
